Permanent LED Lighting Installation in Grand Rapids, MI

Year-round programmable accent lighting for your roofline. Change colors for any holiday or occasion with an app.

Install Once, Enjoy Every Season

Imagine never having to climb a ladder to hang Christmas lights again. With permanent LED lighting from Clean Stream Pro Wash, you get a one-time, professional installation of programmable LED lights along your roofline that you can control from your smartphone year-round. Red and green for Christmas, orange and purple for Halloween, red white and blue for the Fourth of July, your favorite team's colors on game day, or a soft warm white for everyday elegance. The possibilities are truly endless.

Our permanent LED systems are mounted inside a slim, low-profile aluminum channel that attaches directly to your home's trim, fascia, or soffit. The channel is color-matched to blend seamlessly with your roofline, so when the lights are off during the day, they are virtually invisible. Each LED module is individually addressable, meaning you can create custom color zones, chase patterns, fade effects, and even sync your lights to music through the companion smartphone app. It is the same technology used on high-end commercial buildings, now available for residential homes in Grand Rapids and West Michigan.

The LED modules we install are rated for over 50,000 hours, which means more than 15 years of daily evening use. The housing is engineered to withstand Michigan's harsh weather, including heavy snow loads, ice, rain, high winds, and intense summer UV. Unlike seasonal Christmas lights that degrade year after year, permanent LEDs maintain their brightness and color accuracy for their entire lifespan. And because there is nothing to hang or take down, you save time, money, and avoid the safety risks of climbing ladders in cold weather.

Whether you are looking to add architectural accent lighting to a new build, upgrade from seasonal Christmas lights, or enhance your commercial property's nighttime presence, our team handles the entire process. We start with a free on-site consultation to measure your roofline, discuss color preferences and control features, and provide a transparent quote. Installation is typically completed in one day for most residential homes. Once installed, we walk you through the app and make sure you are comfortable controlling every feature of your new lighting system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do permanent LED lights differ from regular Christmas lights?

Permanent LED lights are installed once and stay on your home year-round. They are mounted discreetly along your roofline or trim using a low-profile aluminum channel that blends with your home's architecture. Unlike seasonal Christmas lights, permanent LEDs are app-controlled and fully programmable, allowing you to change colors, patterns, and effects for any holiday, season, game day, or occasion without ever climbing a ladder.

Can I control the lights from my phone?

Yes! Our permanent LED lighting systems come with a smartphone app that gives you full control. You can choose from millions of colors, set custom patterns, schedule on/off times, adjust brightness, and select from pre-built themes for holidays like Christmas, Halloween, Fourth of July, and more. You can even sync your lights to music for parties and special events.

How long do permanent LED lights last?

The LED modules we install are rated for 50,000 or more hours of use, which translates to over 15 years of daily evening operation. The aluminum channel housing is weather-resistant and designed to withstand Michigan's freeze-thaw cycles, heavy snow, rain, and UV exposure without warping, cracking, or fading. We also offer warranties on both the lights and the installation.
