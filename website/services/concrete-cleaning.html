Concrete Cleaning & Sealing in Grand Rapids, MI

Remove stains and protect your concrete with professional cleaning and sealing services.

Restore and Protect Your Concrete Investment

Concrete is one of the most durable building materials around, but it is not invincible. Michigan's brutal freeze-thaw cycles, road salt, oil drips from vehicles, and organic growth like algae and mold all take a toll on your driveway, garage floor, patio, and sidewalks over time. What starts as minor discoloration can eventually lead to surface pitting, cracking, and spalling if left untreated. Clean Stream Pro Wash offers professional concrete cleaning and sealing services that restore your surfaces and protect them for years to come.

Our concrete cleaning process begins with a targeted pre-treatment using professional-grade degreasing agents and organic growth removers. Oil stains, rust spots, tire marks, and algae are treated individually to break down the contaminants before we begin pressure washing. We then use commercial surface cleaners operating at the ideal PSI for concrete to deliver a uniform, streak-free clean across the entire surface. The rotary surface cleaner ensures consistent results without the uneven lines that handheld wands can leave behind.

After cleaning, we offer optional concrete sealing that adds a protective barrier against water penetration, oil absorption, salt damage, and UV fading. This is especially critical in Grand Rapids and West Michigan where the repeated freeze-thaw cycles of winter cause water trapped in concrete pores to expand and contract, leading to surface deterioration. A quality concrete sealer prevents water from entering the pores in the first place, dramatically extending your concrete's lifespan. We offer both matte-finish and wet-look sealers depending on your preference.

Whether you need a one-time deep clean to prepare your home for sale, an annual maintenance wash to keep things looking sharp, or a full clean-and-seal package for long-term protection, our team has you covered. We work with residential driveways, pool decks, patios, commercial parking lots, warehouse floors, and everything in between. Every job includes a thorough inspection, honest assessment, and transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you remove oil stains from my concrete driveway?

Yes, we specialize in oil stain removal from concrete. We use professional-grade degreasing agents that penetrate the concrete's pores to break down and lift oil, transmission fluid, and other automotive stains. Fresh stains are typically removed completely, while older or deeply set stains can be significantly reduced. For the best results, we recommend treating oil stains as soon as possible and following up with a concrete sealer to prevent future absorption.

What does concrete sealing do and is it worth it?

Concrete sealing applies a protective barrier that repels water, oil, road salt, and other contaminants from penetrating the surface. This is especially important in Michigan where freeze-thaw cycles cause water to expand inside concrete pores, leading to cracking, spalling, and pitting. A quality sealer extends the life of your concrete by years, makes future cleaning easier, and can enhance the appearance with a matte or wet-look finish. We recommend resealing every 2-3 years for maximum protection.

How long does concrete sealer take to dry?

Most concrete sealers we apply are dry to the touch within 2-4 hours and ready for foot traffic within 24 hours. We recommend keeping vehicles off the sealed surface for at least 48 hours to allow full curing. Drying times can vary based on temperature, humidity, and the type of sealer used. We always schedule sealing jobs when weather conditions are favorable and will advise you on specific timing for your project.
