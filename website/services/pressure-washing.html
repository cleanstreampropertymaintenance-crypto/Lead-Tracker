Professional Pressure Washing You Can Count On

Over time, Michigan weather takes a toll on your outdoor surfaces. Rain, snow, road salt, dirt, and organic growth can leave your driveway, sidewalks, and patio looking worn and neglected. At Clean Stream Pro Wash, we use professional-grade pressure washing equipment operating between 1,300 and 2,800 PSI to strip away years of buildup and restore your surfaces to like-new condition. Whether you own a home in East Grand Rapids or a commercial property downtown, our team has the equipment and experience to handle jobs of any size.

Our pressure washing service covers a wide range of hard surfaces including concrete driveways, brick pavers, natural stone patios, wooden and composite decks, sidewalks, pool decks, parking lots, and building exteriors. We carefully select the right pressure level, nozzle tip, and cleaning technique for each surface type. Concrete driveways can handle higher PSI, while wooden decks require a gentler approach to prevent splintering and damage. This attention to detail is what sets us apart from other power washing companies near Grand Rapids.

We also offer surface cleaning using specialized rotary attachments that deliver uniform results without the streaky tiger-stripe patterns that handheld wands can leave behind. This is especially important for large flat surfaces like driveways, garage floors, and commercial walkways. The result is an even, consistent clean that dramatically improves your property's curb appeal.

All of our pressure washing services include a pre-treatment to loosen stubborn stains and a post-rinse to leave everything spotless. We take care to protect your landscaping, siding, and vehicles during the process. When we are done, you will see a noticeable difference that can boost your home's value and make your neighbors take notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pressure washing a driveway cost in Grand Rapids?

Driveway pressure washing in Grand Rapids typically ranges from $150 to $350 depending on the size and condition of your driveway. We provide free, no-obligation estimates so you know exactly what to expect before we start. Call us at (616) 366-1647 for a personalized quote.

Will pressure washing damage my concrete or pavers?

When done correctly, pressure washing will not damage concrete or pavers. Our trained technicians adjust the PSI level (between 1300 and 2800) based on the surface material. We also use appropriate nozzle tips and maintain proper distance to ensure a thorough clean without causing etching, chipping, or surface damage.

How often should I have my driveway and sidewalks pressure washed?

In West Michigan, we recommend pressure washing driveways and sidewalks at least once a year, ideally in the spring after winter salt and grime have built up. High-traffic commercial areas may benefit from cleaning every 6 months. Regular cleaning prevents staining, algae growth, and surface deterioration.
