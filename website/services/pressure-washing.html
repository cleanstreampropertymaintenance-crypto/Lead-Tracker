Professional Pressure Washing You Can Count On

Over time, your outdoor surfaces collect dirt, mold, mildew, oil stains, and organic buildup that regular garden hoses simply cannot remove. Clean Stream Pro Wash uses professional-grade pressure washing equipment operating between 1,300 and 2,800 PSI to restore concrete driveways, sidewalks, patios, wooden decks, and commercial surfaces to like-new condition. Based in Caledonia and serving the entire Grand Rapids metro area, we bring the right equipment and expertise to every job.

Our technicians carefully select the correct pressure level for each surface. Concrete driveways and sidewalks can handle higher PSI for deep cleaning, while wooden decks and pavers receive a gentler approach to avoid damage. We use surface cleaners for large flat areas to ensure even, streak-free results, and we always pre-treat tough stains like oil, rust, and tire marks before washing. The result is a thorough, uniform clean that makes your property look years younger.

Michigan winters are tough on outdoor surfaces. Road salt, sand, snowmelt, and freeze-thaw cycles leave behind a layer of grime that accelerates surface deterioration. Annual pressure washing removes these contaminants before they cause permanent damage, saving you money on costly repairs and replacements down the road. Whether you need a single driveway cleaned or an entire commercial parking area restored, we have the capacity and equipment to handle it.

We serve homeowners, property managers, HOAs, and commercial businesses throughout Grand Rapids, Caledonia, Byron Center, Kentwood, Grandville, and all of West Michigan. Every job comes with a free estimate, and we stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pressure washing cost in Grand Rapids?

Pressure washing costs in Grand Rapids typically range from $150 to $500 depending on the size of the area and the type of surface. Driveways average $150-$300, while full patios and decks may run $200-$400. We provide free, no-obligation estimates for every job.

Will pressure washing damage my concrete or deck?

When done correctly, pressure washing will not damage your surfaces. Our technicians adjust the PSI (1300-2800) based on the material being cleaned. Softer surfaces like wood decks get lower pressure, while concrete driveways can handle higher pressure. We always test a small area first.

How often should I have my driveway pressure washed?

We recommend pressure washing your driveway once a year, ideally in the spring after winter salt and grime have built up. High-traffic commercial surfaces may benefit from cleaning every 6 months. Regular cleaning prevents permanent staining and extends the life of your concrete.
