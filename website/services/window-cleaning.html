Professional Window Cleaning for Homes & Businesses

Clean windows make a bigger difference than most people expect. They let in more natural light, improve your view, and instantly make your home or business look more polished and well-maintained. At Clean Stream Pro Wash, we provide thorough, streak-free window cleaning for residential and commercial properties throughout Grand Rapids, Caledonia, Byron Center, and all of West Michigan.

Our window cleaning service goes far beyond a quick spray and wipe. We clean every part of the window, including the glass on both the interior and exterior sides, the window frames, the tracks, and the sills. We also remove and clean window screens, clearing away the dust, pollen, and debris that accumulate over the seasons. For homes with storm windows, we can disassemble and clean each layer for a truly complete result.

Hard water stains are one of the most common window problems we see in West Michigan. Sprinkler systems, mineral-rich rain runoff, and even condensation can leave cloudy white spots that regular cleaning cannot remove. Our team uses professional-grade mineral deposit removers and polishing compounds to restore glass clarity without scratching. We have successfully restored windows that homeowners thought needed to be replaced, saving them hundreds or even thousands of dollars.

Whether you need a one-time deep clean before a special event, a seasonal refresh, or a regular maintenance plan, we can customize a window cleaning package that fits your needs and budget. Our technicians are trained, insured, and equipped with the right tools to safely clean windows on multi-story homes and commercial buildings. We use purified water systems, professional squeegees, and microfiber detailing to deliver a spotless, streak-free finish every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean both the inside and outside of windows?

Yes! We offer both interior and exterior window cleaning as a complete package or separately. Our interior service includes careful protection of your floors and furnishings. We also clean window screens, tracks, and sills as part of our standard service so every part of your window is spotless.

Can you remove hard water stains from my windows?

Absolutely. Hard water stains are a common issue in West Michigan, often caused by sprinkler overspray or mineral-rich runoff. We use professional-grade hard water stain removal compounds that safely dissolve mineral deposits without scratching the glass. For severe buildup, we may use a multi-step restoration process to bring your windows back to full clarity.

How often should I schedule professional window cleaning?

For most homes in Grand Rapids, we recommend professional window cleaning twice a year - once in the spring and once in the fall. Homes near busy roads, construction sites, or with lots of trees may benefit from quarterly cleanings. Commercial properties often schedule monthly or bi-monthly service to maintain a professional appearance.
