The Safe Way to Clean Your Home's Exterior

Not every surface can handle the force of a pressure washer. Vinyl siding, painted wood, stucco, EIFS, and asphalt shingles can all be damaged or driven with water behind them by high-pressure cleaning. That is where soft washing comes in. Clean Stream Pro Wash uses a low-pressure application (under 500 PSI) paired with professional-grade, biodegradable cleaning solutions to safely remove algae, mold, mildew, lichen, and dirt from your home's exterior without any risk of damage.

Our soft wash process does more than just clean the surface. The specialized solutions we apply penetrate organic growth and kill it at the root, which means your home stays cleaner far longer than it would after a standard pressure wash. Green and black streaks on your siding, dark stains on your roof, and fuzzy mold patches around your gutters are all signs of organic growth that responds extremely well to soft washing. Most homes in West Michigan develop these issues due to our humid summers and shaded lots.

We follow a careful process on every house wash. First, we protect your landscaping by pre-wetting plants and covering delicate areas. Then we apply our cleaning solution from the bottom up, allowing it to dwell and break down contaminants. Finally, we rinse everything thoroughly from the top down, leaving behind a spotless finish. The entire process is safe for your siding, roof, windows, landscaping, and pets.

Whether you have a small ranch home in Kentwood or a large two-story in East Grand Rapids, our team has the equipment and experience to handle it. We also soft wash commercial buildings, HOA communities, and multi-unit properties. Every job includes a free estimate and our satisfaction guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing relies on high-pressure water (1300-2800 PSI) to blast away dirt and is best for hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure (under 500 PSI) combined with biodegradable cleaning solutions that kill mold, algae, and bacteria at the root. Soft washing is the safe choice for siding, roofs, stucco, and painted surfaces that could be damaged by high pressure.

Is soft washing safe for my landscaping and pets?

Yes. We use biodegradable, plant-friendly cleaning solutions. We also pre-wet your landscaping before treatment and rinse it afterward as an extra precaution. Our solutions break down quickly and pose no risk to pets, children, or plants once the treated surfaces are dry.

How long do soft washing results last?

Because soft washing kills algae, mold, and mildew at the root rather than just blasting it off the surface, results typically last 2-3 times longer than pressure washing alone. Most homes stay clean for 1-3 years depending on shade, humidity, and proximity to trees.
