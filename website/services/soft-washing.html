The Safe Way to Clean Your Home's Exterior

Not every surface can handle the force of a pressure washer. Vinyl siding, painted wood, stucco, EIFS, and asphalt shingles can all be damaged by high-pressure water. That is where soft washing comes in. Clean Stream Pro Wash uses a low-pressure technique (under 500 PSI) combined with professional-strength, biodegradable cleaning solutions to safely remove algae, mold, mildew, lichen, and dirt from your home's exterior without any risk of damage.

Our soft wash process works by applying a specially formulated cleaning solution that kills organic growth at its root. Unlike pressure washing, which only blasts away the surface layer and allows regrowth within weeks, soft washing delivers results that last three to four times longer. The solution breaks down the cellular structure of algae, mold, and mildew so they do not come back quickly. After the solution has done its work, we rinse everything with low-pressure water, leaving your home looking brand new.

Soft washing is ideal for full house washes, including vinyl siding, Hardie board, cedar shakes, brick, stone veneer, stucco, and painted surfaces of all types. We also use the soft wash method on roofs to safely remove the black streaks caused by Gloeocapsa magma algae, a common problem on Michigan homes. Left untreated, this algae can shorten your roof's lifespan and increase energy costs by darkening the surface.

Every soft wash service from Clean Stream Pro Wash includes a thorough pre-inspection, plant and landscaping protection, application of our eco-friendly cleaning solution, and a complete rinse. We serve homeowners across Grand Rapids, Caledonia, Byron Center, Kentwood, Grandville, and all of West Michigan. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing relies on high water pressure (1300-2800 PSI) to blast away dirt and grime from hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure (under 500 PSI) combined with specially formulated, biodegradable cleaning solutions that kill algae, mold, mildew, and bacteria at the root. Soft washing is the safe choice for delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, stucco, roofs, and painted wood.

Is soft washing safe for my landscaping and pets?

Yes. We use biodegradable, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for your landscaping, pets, and family. We also pre-rinse all nearby plants and landscaping before and after the wash to provide an extra layer of protection. Our technicians are trained to apply solutions carefully and responsibly.

How long does a house wash take in Grand Rapids?

A typical residential house wash in Grand Rapids takes between 1.5 and 3 hours depending on the size of your home, the number of stories, and the level of buildup on the siding. Most single-family homes are completed in about 2 hours. We handle everything from pre-treatment to final rinse so you can sit back and watch the transformation.
