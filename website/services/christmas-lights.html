Christmas Light Installation in Grand Rapids, MI

Full-service holiday lighting: custom design, professional installation, maintenance, takedown, and storage.

Make Your Home the Best on the Block This Holiday Season

There is nothing quite like a beautifully lit home during the holidays. But the reality of hanging Christmas lights yourself - climbing ladders in freezing temperatures, untangling old strands, replacing burnt-out bulbs, and trying to make everything look even - is far less magical. Clean Stream Pro Wash takes all of that off your plate with our full-service Christmas light installation for homes and businesses in Grand Rapids and throughout West Michigan.

Our process starts with a free design consultation where we visit your property, discuss your vision, and create a custom lighting plan tailored to your home's architecture. Whether you want a classic warm white roofline display, a colorful multi-color extravaganza, or something in between, we will design a layout that highlights your home's best features. We use only commercial-grade LED lights that are brighter, more energy-efficient, and far more durable than anything you will find at a retail store. These lights are rated to last 50,000 hours or more and can handle the harshest Michigan winter weather.

Installation day is completely hands-off for you. Our trained crew arrives with all lights, clips, extension cords, timers, and hardware needed for a clean, professional installation. We use non-damaging clips that attach to your roofline, gutters, windows, and trim without leaving holes or marks. Every strand is tested before installation, and we set everything up on a timer so your lights turn on and off automatically each night.

Our service does not end on installation day. We include full-season maintenance at no extra charge, so if a bulb goes out or a strand comes loose, we will fix it promptly. After the holiday season, we return to carefully remove all lights, and we offer optional storage so everything is organized and ready for next year. Many of our customers sign up for our annual program, which locks in their pricing and guarantees an early installation date each season.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I book Christmas light installation in Grand Rapids?

We recommend booking your Christmas light installation by mid-September to early October. Our schedule fills up fast once the holiday season approaches, and early booking ensures you get your preferred installation date. We typically begin installations in late October and continue through early December, weather permitting.

Do you provide the lights or do I need to buy my own?

We provide everything. Our service includes commercial-grade LED lights, all necessary clips, extension cords, timers, and installation hardware. Commercial-grade LEDs are brighter, more energy-efficient, and far more durable than store-bought lights. They are rated to last 50,000+ hours and can withstand harsh Michigan winters without fading or failing.

What happens if some lights go out during the season?

Our Christmas light installation service includes a full-season maintenance guarantee. If any lights go out, a strand fails, or a section comes loose due to wind or weather, simply call us and we will come out to fix it at no additional charge. We want your display to look perfect from the day we install it until the day we take it down.
